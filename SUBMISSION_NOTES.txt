Method summary:
TIDE-Mem stores immutable raw conversational evidence together with gpt-4o-mini structured memory cards. It maintains event-time-aware mutable state, applies exact user_id isolation, and combines FTS5/exact/temporal retrieval, reciprocal-rank fusion, evidence-ID-only gpt-4o-mini reranking, and coverage-aware selection. Search returns only stored evidence and never generates the final answer.
